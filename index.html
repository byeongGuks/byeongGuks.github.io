Byeongguk Jeon

M.S. student at KAIST AI · Researcher at Config Intelligence

I am an M.S. student at the Kim Jaechul Graduate School of AI, KAIST, advised by Prof. Minjoon Seo, and a researcher at Config Intelligence, a robotics startup building robot foundation models. I will continue at KAIST AI as a Ph.D. student starting March 2027.

My research focuses on video world models for robot manipulation: using generative video models as neural simulators for evaluating generalist robot policies, and as engines for converting human videos into robot training data.

Previously, I worked on retrieval-augmented and latent reasoning with LLMs at DMIS Lab (Korea University) and LK Lab (KAIST), and was an AI researcher and engineer at NAVER. I received my B.S. in Computer Science and Engineering from Korea University.
